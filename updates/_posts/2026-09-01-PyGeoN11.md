We released version 1.1 of [PyGeoN](https://github.com/compgeo-mox/pygeon) today. This release contains
- Unified mass matrix assembly using the inclusion maps to piecewise polynomials.
- Finite volume methods: TPSA for elasticity and TPFA for flow.
- Generalized Poincaré operators on domains with non-trivial topology.
